LSEG Agrees to Buy Credit-Data Provider Octus for $3.54 Billion

The all-cash, debt-financed deal adds private and distressed credit intelligence to LSEG's data business. LSEG says it will lift its 2027 subscription growth outlook, and it expects the deal to close in the first half of 2027.

Read more

London Stock Exchange Group agreed on October 22 to buy New York-based Octus for $3.535 billion in cash. The sellers are funds advised by Permira and Octus management shareholders, including founder and CEO Kent Collier. LSEG will pay with external debt and expects to close in the first half of 2027, subject to regulatory approval. Octus specializes in private credit, distressed debt and less liquid fixed income. LSEG plans to combine it with IFR news, evaluated pricing, Yield Book analytics and LPC loan data to cover the full credit investment process. The deal also gives LSEG better access to hedge funds and alternative asset managers. LSEG expects it to raise its outlook of about 7% organic subscription revenue growth in 2027 and is keeping its medium-term EBITDA guidance.

Impact on the tech industry: Financial-data vendors are racing to own private-credit data, which AI analytics and agent workflows need. The deal adds pressure on Bloomberg, S&P Global and Moody's, and it confirms that proprietary data is the scarce asset in AI. Watch antitrust review and further private-credit data deals.
