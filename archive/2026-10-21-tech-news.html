Salesforce to Acquire Braze for $5.66 Billion

Salesforce will pay $47.50 per share in cash for the customer-engagement platform, its largest marketing-side bet since ExactTarget, to fold real-time messaging into Marketing Cloud and Agentforce.

Read more

Salesforce signed a definitive agreement to buy Braze at an enterprise value of about $5.66 billion, with Class A shareholders receiving $47.50 per share in cash. Braze shares jumped more than 50% on the news. Braze serves roughly 2,770 customers, including Canva, Instacart, Venmo and Urban Outfitters, across mobile, email, web, SMS and WhatsApp.

Closing is expected in Salesforce's fiscal 2028, which begins February 1, 2027, subject to shareholder approval and regulatory clearance. Co-founder Bill Magnuson is expected to stay on as CEO, and existing customer contracts remain in effect. Reported termination fees are $300 million payable by Salesforce and $191.4 million payable by Braze.

Impact on the tech industry: The deal signals that enterprise vendors will buy, not build, the real-time customer data and messaging layer that AI agents need. Independent marketing-automation vendors such as Adobe, HubSpot and Iterable now face a better-funded rival, and Braze customers who also use competing clouds should watch for changes to interoperability and pricing. Integration risk is real: Salesforce must merge a second engagement stack with Marketing Cloud without disrupting customers.

Microsoft Adds "Guardian Agents" to Police Enterprise AI Agents

New controls treat AI agents as potential insider threats, with real-time risk scoring, human-approval gates and the power to shut an agent down.

Read more

Microsoft expanded Project Perception and Microsoft 365 E7 with Guardian Agents that assess each agent's risk in real time. Administrators can restrict actions, require human approval for high-impact changes, or isolate an agent entirely. Discovery now covers about 60 types of locally installed agents, including Claude Code, OpenAI Codex and GitHub Copilot, and agents start with minimal, time-bound permissions. Capabilities are at different stages of availability.

Impact on the tech industry: Agent governance is becoming its own product category. Identity, endpoint and security vendors will race to offer agent inventory and permission controls, and buyers will start asking every agent platform, including Salesforce Agentforce and ServiceNow, how it handles agent identity and containment.

AI Agents Used to Breach Over 100 Company Websites at About $50 Each

A researcher report says one operator directed autonomous agents on an open-source platform to attack more than 1,200 sites, with little human effort.

Read more

A report from NorForge researcher Snorre Fagerland, reviewed by Reuters, describes a Chinese-speaking operator who used agents on the open-source Cairn platform to probe sites across the US, Canada, Australia and Japan. Impact on the tech industry: It is concrete evidence that agentic tools lower the cost and skill needed for broad intrusion campaigns. Expect faster patch-cycle expectations, more investment in AI-driven defense, and tighter scrutiny of open-source agent platforms.
